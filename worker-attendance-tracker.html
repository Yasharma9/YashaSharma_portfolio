How the system works

Built in Google Sheets with Apps Script for contract workers across several farm sites. Each site keeps its own register; HR keeps one worker master; a compiled dashboard reads every site.

Checks while typing

  • One day per person per date. Full day = 1, half day = 0.5. A worker can never total more than one day on a date, across all rows, locations and sites.
  • No repeat location. The same worker can't be entered twice at one location on one day.
  • Location required. Needed for a full or half day, empty for an absence. A worker who moves during the day is recorded at both places, half a day each.
  • Inside the employment window. No attendance before the joining date or after the leaving date.
  • Picked, not typed. Workers are chosen from a list of registered IDs and names, so look-alike names are never mixed up.

Checks on the worker record

  • One identity. Aadhaar is the unique identity: one number can't sit under two people, and names may repeat.
  • Complete and valid KYC. 12-digit Aadhaar, valid bank account and IFSC, age 18 or over, home state that matches local or outside.
  • Rejoining rules. Records of one person can't overlap. After "Removed" a worker may rejoin the next day, after "Exit" only after 30 days, and "Black listed" never.
  • New workers without Aadhaar get a short grace period, then the row is flagged.

Checks for HR across sites

  • Automatic Worker ID. When a site registers a worker, a script asks the HR master for an ID within seconds, so every site uses the same ID for the same person.
  • Possible duplicates. Same name and father or husband name raises a warning that HR can confirm or override.
  • Active elsewhere. A worker still active at another site can't be registered again until the old site releases them.
  • Two sites, one date. People marked at two sites on the same day are listed for HR to review.

Definitions

Mandays
Full days + 0.5 × half days.
Attendance %
Mandays ÷ days marked (full, half or absent). Unmarked days are left out and counted separately.
Avg mandays per worker
Mandays ÷ workers who worked at least one day in the month.
Avg daily manpower
Mandays ÷ days in the month.
Workers on roll
Workers whose joining and leaving dates cover any day of the month.
Unmarked day
A working day between joining and leaving with no entry at all.
All workers, contractors, sites and numbers on this page are generated with a fixed random seed in JavaScript. They do not come from any farm or company, and no real worker data is used.
