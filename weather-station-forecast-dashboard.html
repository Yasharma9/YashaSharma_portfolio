How to read this: solid lines are station readings and dashed lines with a shaded band are the forecast. The forecast in this sample is simulated as a blend of the station’s own recent daily pattern, which counts most in the first hours, and a bias-corrected outside forecast, which counts more later in the week. The shaded band shows the likely range and widens with time. Absolute humidity, wet-bulb temperature, dew point, DLI, rain intensity and gust are calculated from the recorded values. All data is randomly generated.
