How to read this: solid lines are readings, dashed lines are the 7-day forecast and the shaded band is its likely range. The forecast starts from the seasonal normal for each date, plus today’s departure from normal, fading over about four days. The range is based on how far the last 30 days strayed from normal. The growth index is an illustrative 0 to 100 score from temperature, light and VPD. All data is randomly generated.
