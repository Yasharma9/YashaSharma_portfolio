Data

All values are randomly generated for this demo, with faults added on purpose: sensors that went quiet or got stuck, entries that are missing, repeated, mistyped or out of line, and slow drift. The checks run on that data in your browser, so changing a setting changes the results. Crop records follow the same crop curves as the farm KPI dashboard. Time saved assumes the minutes per parameter entered in the settings.
