How each value is calculated
  • Saturation vapour pressure: es = 0.61094 × exp(17.625 × T ÷ (T + 243.04)), in kPa.
  • Actual vapour pressure: ea = es × RH ÷ 100. Air VPD = es − ea.
  • Leaf VPD uses es at the leaf temperature (air temperature minus the offset) minus the same ea.
  • Dew point: the temperature at which es equals ea.
  • Wet-bulb temperature: solved from es(Tw) − 0.000662 × P × (T − Tw) = ea, for a ventilated psychrometer.
  • Absolute humidity = 2166.8 × ea ÷ (T + 273.15), in g/m³. Humidity deficit is the same at saturation minus the actual value.
  • Humidity ratio = 0.622 × ea ÷ (P − ea). Air density is the dry air part plus the water vapour part, from the gas laws.
  • Enthalpy = 1.006 × T + w × (2501 + 1.86 × T), in kJ per kg of dry air.

The VPD bands are general guidance for greenhouse crops. The best range depends on the crop and its growth stage, so use them as a starting point.
